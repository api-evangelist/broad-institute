Broad Institute is a nonprofit biomedical research center dedicated to improving human health through advanced research, tool development, and open data sharing. It brings together a multidisciplinary community of scientists, engineers, and clinicians to accelerate discoveries and translate them into therapies, diagnostics, and public health solutions.

**Areas:** Biotechnology · Research · Genomics · Open Science · Non-Profit

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Broad Institute: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
